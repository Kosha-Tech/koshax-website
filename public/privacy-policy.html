KoshaX Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 13, 2026 · Last Updated: February 13, 2026
KoshaX is a privacy-first AI assistant and personal data platform. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use KoshaX. In this policy, "Services" refers to the KoshaX application, website, APIs, and any related features or tools we provide.
1. Principles 2. Data We Collect 3. Email Integration 4. How We Use Data 5. AI Processing 6. Data Ownership 7. Security 8. Retention 9. Your Rights 10. Transfers 11. Children 12. Changes 13. Contact

1. Our Privacy Principles

User ownership of data
Minimal collection
Security by design
No sale of personal data
No advertising-based monetization

We do not sell personal data. We do not monetize user data via advertising.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Account credentials
  • Messages, prompts, notes, and uploads (text, images, audio, files)
  • Contacts (if you grant permission)
  • Calendar information (if integrated)
  • Email data (if connected)
  • Health metrics (if voluntarily entered)
  • Preferences and settings

2.2 Automatically collected information

  • Device type and operating system
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Usage logs and interaction events
  • Crash diagnostics and performance data

This data is used for security, performance, and reliability.

2.3 Third-party integrations

If you connect third-party services (such as Google, Apple, WhatsApp, Telegram, or others), KoshaX may receive only the data you explicitly authorize, such as basic profile information and authorized contacts/calendar data.

3. Email Integration (If Connected)

Email integration is optional and requires explicit OAuth authorization.

3.1 Scope of access

  • Email metadata (sender, subject, timestamp)
  • Email body content (only when required for user-requested actions)
  • Labels or folders (if needed for organization features)

Access is limited to the scopes you grant, and we request the minimum scope necessary.

3.2 Purpose of processing

  • Generate summaries
  • Draft replies upon request
  • Extract reminders, tasks, or calendar events
  • Enable intelligent search
  • Provide contextual AI assistance

3.3 AI processing of email data

If you request AI actions (such as summarization), relevant email content may be securely processed by trusted AI providers solely to generate the output you requested. We do not permit third-party AI providers to use your email data for public model training, advertising, or independent commercial use.

3.4 Storage and control

Email data may be processed transiently or securely stored if needed for features (such as memory, reminders, or task tracking). You can disconnect email access at any time. Upon disconnection, access tokens are revoked and no new email data is accessed. Stored derived data may be deleted upon request.

3.5 Google API compliance (if applicable)

KoshaX's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide AI-powered assistance
  • Deliver reminders, scheduling, and productivity tools
  • Store and organize user data
  • Personalize experiences
  • Improve reliability and performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal data.

5. AI Model Processing

KoshaX uses AI systems to generate responses and assist users. Content you submit may be processed by internal AI systems and/or trusted third-party model providers, solely to provide requested functionality.

We do not use user content for public model training without explicit consent. Where available, we may offer options for private or local processing.

6. Data Ownership

You retain ownership of all content you upload or generate through KoshaX. KoshaX acts as a service provider processing data on your behalf to deliver the Services.

7. Data Storage and Security

  • Encryption in transit (TLS)
  • Encryption at rest
  • Access control and authentication mechanisms
  • Infrastructure monitoring
  • Audit logging
  • Secure cloud hosting

Sensitive tokens and credentials are encrypted. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized systems and personnel.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the Services, maintain accounts, comply with legal requirements, and resolve disputes.

You may request deletion of your account by contacting privacy@koshax.com. Upon verified request, personal data will be deleted unless legally required to retain it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws (including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and other global regulations), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, withdraw consent, restrict processing, and request portability of your data.

To exercise these rights, contact privacy@koshax.com.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your residence. We implement appropriate safeguards consistent with applicable data protection laws.

11. Children's Privacy

KoshaX is not intended for individuals under 13 years of age (or applicable local age threshold). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be reflected with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of KoshaX constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
